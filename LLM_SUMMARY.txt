================================================================================
                        WHAT THE LLM ACTUALLY DOES
================================================================================

The LLM performs TECHNICAL ANALYSIS, not just sentiment!

Inputs to LLM:
  [x] Current price
  [x] 24-hour price change
  [x] Price trend (uptrend/downtrend/sideways)
  [x] Recent vs older moving averages
  [ ] Optional: Social sentiment

Output from LLM:
  -> Trading action (BUY/SELL/HOLD)
  -> Confidence level (0.0 to 1.0)
  -> Reasoning (in metadata)

Example LLM Prompt:
  "Analyze this crypto market data and provide a trading signal:
   Pair: SOL/USD
   Current Price: $157.26
   24h Change: -0.25%
   Price Trend: sideways
   
   Based on this data, should I BUY, SELL, or HOLD?"
